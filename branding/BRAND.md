# Angleprobe brand assets

- `angleprobe-logo-lockup.png` is the primary horizontal logo on a transparent background.
- `../icons/icon.svg` is the clean, vector extension mark and source for the PNG icon sizes.

Core palette:

- Deep navy: `#14213D`
- Teal: `#18A999`
- Accent blue: `#4C7DFF`
- Soft canvas: `#F7FAFC`

Keep generous clear space around the mark. Do not add political symbols, flags, newspaper imagery, an eye, or a checkmark; Angleprobe should communicate neutral inspection rather than a verdict.
